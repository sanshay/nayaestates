About Naya Estates

Capital With A Long-Term View.

Naya Estates is a trust-based investment company focused on ownership, partnership and value creation across technology and real estate.

Our Identity

Built For Enduring Ownership.

We approach investment as stewardship. That means seeking quality over volume, understanding what drives long-term value, and aligning ourselves with operators and partners capable of executing through market cycles.

Our two core sectors—Technology and Real Estate—offer distinct but complementary ways to compound capital. Real estate provides tangible asset value and durable income potential. Technology provides access to innovation, productivity gains and scalable growth.
