Investment Strategy

Conviction Before Capital.

Our investment process is designed to balance ambition with discipline—focusing on quality, downside awareness and the ability to compound over time.

Core Philosophy

We Do Not Invest To Follow A Cycle.

We seek opportunities where value can be created through fundamentals rather than momentum. That means understanding the asset, market, management team, capital requirements and realistic path to value creation before investment.

We are comfortable being selective. A smaller number of high-conviction investments allows deeper diligence, greater alignment and more meaningful support after capital is deployed.

Investment Process

A Structured Path To Conviction.

01

Origination

We source opportunities through relationships, sector networks, operators, developers and direct engagement.

02

Initial Screening

Strategic fit, market attractiveness, capital need, return potential and downside characteristics.

03

Deep Diligence

Commercial, financial, technical, legal and operational analysis appropriate to the investment.

04

Structuring

Investment terms designed to align risk, ownership, governance and long-term incentives.

05

Investment Decision

Capital is deployed only where our conviction meets the required risk and return discipline.

06

Portfolio Stewardship

Ongoing review, strategic support and disciplined decisions as conditions evolve.

What We Look For

The Characteristics Of A Naya Investment.

Structural Demand

Clear long-term demand drivers rather than temporary market excitement.

Quality & Differentiation

Strong assets, technology, intellectual property, location or execution capability.

Aligned Operators

Leadership teams with credibility, ownership mindset and execution discipline.

Defensible Economics

Attractive unit economics, asset economics or a credible pathway to them.

Downside Awareness

Risks that can be understood, structured and monitored with discipline.

Long-Term Optionality

Room for expansion, reinvestment, platform growth or asset appreciation.
